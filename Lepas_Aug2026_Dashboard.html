    Reading note. Bookings are last click, in-platform. View-through and assisted conversions are excluded from every figure here to avoid double counting. Demographic, placement and geographic breakdowns are separate platform aggregates: they reconcile to the same channel totals but cannot be cross-tabbed with creative. Est. pipeline applies a 64% test-drive attendance rate and a 21% close rate to qualified bookings; both are planning assumptions, not dealer actuals.

    On the model split. The L4 range is named for big cats (AMUR, JAVAN, PANTERA), and the media plan follows the same lines: a dedicated campaign per trim, plus a range campaign carrying the retargeting pools. Most model and audience combinations never ran together, so those filter pairs are struck through.
