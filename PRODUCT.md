# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two readers open the same document and want different things from it.

**Lepas SA marketing lead (client-side).** Opens it before a meeting, skims, and needs the
verdict in the first screen: are we hitting cost per test drive, is the money working, what
do I put in front of the exec. Rarely scrolls to ad-set level. Will be asked to defend every
number by someone who did not read the report.

**Agency performance team.** Lives in the detail. Needs ad-set CPLs, creative fatigue curves,
placement exclusions and audience efficiency, in a form they can act on Monday morning. Will
cross-check anything that looks wrong against the platform export.

The job to be done: turn a 2,551-row media export into a document that survives being
forwarded, without either reader needing a second version.

## Product Purpose

Monthly performance reporting for Lepas South Africa's paid media, where the single
conversion is a booked test drive at lepas-sa.co.za/offers.

The report exists to **prove the campaign is working** and to withstand scrutiny when it does.
Success is the client forwarding it unedited to Chery/OEM stakeholders and no one coming back
asking what a number means or where it came from.

## Brand Personality

**Bold. Youthful. Design-led.**

Lepas is Chery's design-forward SUV brand. Its own language is confident and styling-led:
"Drive a LEPAS today", "bold design and big tech", "feline poise". The public model menu uses
L4 ICE, L6 ICE, L6 EV and L8 (coming soon). Older workbook and campaign shorthand can still
appear as AMUR, JAVAN, PANTERA or Range, but the report should translate those into public
LEPAS model language wherever a client will read it.

Voice in this document: direct and unhedged. State the number, then what it means. No
throat-clearing, no "it appears that". Where something underperformed, say so plainly, the
credibility of the good news depends on it.

The report should be recognisably a Lepas artefact, not a platform export with a logo on it.

## Anti-references

All four are explicitly ruled out:

- **Default Looker Studio / GA4 report.** Stock blue-and-grey template, scorecard tiles,
  donut charts, Roboto throughout. The report every agency sends. Reads as effort not spent.
- **Dark "crypto-trader" analytics panel.** Near-black with neon green, glowing bars. The
  first-order category reflex for anything called a dashboard.
- **Generic SaaS marketing dashboard.** Purple-to-blue gradient hero, one giant metric on a
  card, uniform rounded corners, icon-and-heading card grids.
- **Dense enterprise BI grid.** Wall-to-wall tables, no hierarchy, 11px grey text, filters
  buried in dropdowns. Powerful and unreadable.

Second-order trap to avoid as well: "a dashboard that is none of the above" defaults to
light editorial-typographic. Being merely restrained is not the answer here; the brief is
bold.

## Design Principles

1. **The verdict before the evidence.** The first screen answers "is it working?" in words,
   not just figures. Detail is earned by scrolling, never front-loaded.
2. **Every mark is defensible.** Each number on screen is one someone may have to justify to
   an OEM. No ornamental data, no chart that exists to fill a slot, no metric without a
   stated definition.
3. **Design-led, not template-led.** This is a Lepas document. If it could carry another
   brand's logo unchanged, it has failed.
4. **Two readers, one document.** The client skims and the agency digs. Hierarchy does the
   work of separating them, not two exports.
5. **Show the limits.** Where the data cannot support a cut, say so on the surface:
   separate platform aggregates, attribution overlap, modelled assumptions. Stated limits
   are why the rest is trusted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.2 AA** across contrast, focus visibility, keyboard operation and semantics.
- **Colour-vision safe charts are a hard requirement.** Every categorical palette is
  validated for CVD separation before shipping, not eyeballed. Adjacent-pair separation is
  held above the documented target in both light and dark themes.
- **Colour is never the only channel.** Every chart carries direct labels and a table-view
  twin; series identity is available as text.
- **Reduced motion** is honoured; no animation is required to read a value.
- Charts and tables must remain legible at 200% zoom without horizontal page scroll.
